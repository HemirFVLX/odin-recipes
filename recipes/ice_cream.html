How to make ice cream

The information below shows the ingredients and steps needed to make delicious ice cream:

  • 2 cups heavy whipped cream
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract (you can use any other flavor such as chocolate or strawberry)
  • A small pinch of salt
  1. Whip the cream
  2. Mix the base (condensed milk, vanilla extract, salt) in a bowl
  3. Gently combine the base with the whipped cream
  4. Let the mixture inside a freezer for 4-6 hours
  5. Serve on a bowl or a cone (if you have one)
