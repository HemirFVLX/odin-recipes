How to make a pizza

The information below shows the ingredients and steps needed to make a good pizza:

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Ham
  • Pepperoni
  • Green peppers
  • Onions
  1. Flatten the pizza dough
  2. Add tomato sauce
  3. Add cheese
  4. Add the other ingredients
  5. Bake at a temperature of 220 °C for 15 minutes
  6. Done!
