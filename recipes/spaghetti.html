How to make Spaghetti Bolognese

The information below shows the ingredients and steps needed to make good pasta:

  • Spaghetti (no. 5)
  • Tomato sauce
  • Minced beef
  • Onions
  • Parmesan cheese
  1. Boil the spaghetti for about 10 minutes
  2. Carefully remove the water from your cooking pot where your pasta is (don't forget to leave some!)
  3. Put the sauce, minced beef and onions into the pot
  4. Carefully stir for around a minute
  5. Serve with some Parmesan cheese shredded above
